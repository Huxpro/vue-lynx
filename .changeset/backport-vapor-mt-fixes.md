Backport branch-independent fixes from the `vapor` line. `INIT_MT_REF` now overwrites an existing `MainThreadRef` entry instead of skipping it, so the background thread's initial value — authoritative during IFR hydration — is no longer dropped in favor of the main-thread first-screen value, and `resetWorkletState()` actually clears the worklet ref registry on page reset and IFR fallback. IFR hydration treats a diverging list platform attribute (`item-key`, `estimated-main-axis-size-px`, …) as unrepairable and rebuilds, since those reach native through `update-list-info` at insert time and a later `SET_PROP` cannot repair the row native was already told about; a throwing hydration patch now falls back to the background replay instead of escaping `vuePatchUpdate`. On the IFR main thread, an unavailable ops sink drops the batch with a dev warning rather than falling through to `callLepusMethod` and re-entering `vuePatchUpdate` as if it came from the background thread. `runOnMainThread` no longer performs background exec-map bookkeeping in the main-thread realm, the flush ack tolerates realms without `setTimeout`, `lynx` is probed with `typeof` before use, and `worklet-loader` shares `hasMainThreadDirective` with `worklet-loader-mt`.
